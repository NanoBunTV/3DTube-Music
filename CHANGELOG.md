# Changelog

## v1.0.0

First release of 3DTube Music, a YouTube Music client for the Nintendo 3DS, based on FourthTube Beta 34.1.

- Audio-only playback, no ads, SponsorBlock auto-skip (non-music sections, sponsors, self-promotion,
  interaction reminders)
- YouTube Music home feed and search (songs, albums, artists, playlists)
- Album, playlist and artist pages
- Now-playing screen with album art, lyrics, play queue, shuffle and repeat
- Radio : keeps playing similar songs when the queue ends
- Optional sign-in : Library tab with your Liked Music and playlists, like songs (saved to your YouTube Liked
  videos), add songs to playlists or create new ones
- Listening history
- Interface in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese and Japanese
- In-app updates from this repository's releases (Settings > Update)

Install : copy `3DTubeMusic.3dsx` to `sd:/3ds/` and open it from the Homebrew Launcher, or install
`3DTubeMusic.cia` with FBI. Requires Luma3DS and dumped DSP firmware.

Known limitations : tested on a New 3DS only; signing in with an unofficial client can get an account flagged, so
consider a secondary account.
